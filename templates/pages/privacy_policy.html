{{ CHURCH_NAME }} ("we", "us", or "our") operates this Sermon Q&A platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our service to submit questions about sermons. We are committed to protecting your privacy and operating transparently.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information necessary to operate the service:

  • Question text — the content of the question you submit.
  • Selected question category — optional label you choose (e.g. "Clarification", "Prayer Request").
  • IP address — recorded solely to enforce rate limits (max 5 questions per hour per IP) and prevent spam. It is never displayed publicly or shared with speakers.
  • Submission timestamp — the date and time your question was submitted.
We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, WhatsApp ID, social media profile, or any other personal identifier. No account or login is required.

2. How Anonymity Works

When you submit a question, it is forwarded to the sermon speaker's WhatsApp as an anonymous message. The message the speaker receives contains:

  • The sermon title and date
  • Your question text
  • A note that it was submitted anonymously via the platform

The speaker cannot see your name, phone number, IP address, or any identifying information. The message is sent from our church's WhatsApp Business account, not from you personally. Your identity is fully protected at the technical level.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information collected only for the following purposes:

  • Delivering your question — forwarding it anonymously to the relevant sermon speaker via WhatsApp.
  • Rate limiting — using your IP address to prevent spam submissions (5 questions per hour).
  • Platform analytics — aggregated, non-identifiable statistics (e.g. total questions per sermon, delivery rates) shown only to church administrators to help improve the service.
  • Troubleshooting — diagnosing delivery failures and retrying failed WhatsApp sends.

We do not use your information for marketing, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating this service.

4. WhatsApp Delivery

Questions are delivered using the Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API. When a question is sent, it passes through Meta's servers as a business-initiated message. Meta's own privacy policy governs how they process message data in transit. The speaker's WhatsApp number is stored in our system solely for the purpose of routing your anonymous question to the correct person.

Meta's privacy policy is available at whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy .

5. Data Retention

Questions and associated metadata (submission time, delivery status, IP address) are retained in our database for as long as they are operationally useful — typically the lifetime of the sermon they relate to. Administrators may delete questions at any time from the admin panel.

IP addresses used for rate limiting are stored temporarily in cache memory and automatically expire after 1 hour. They are not written to permanent storage independently.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

This platform uses only essential, functional cookies:

  • Session cookie — used only if you are an authenticated administrator. Anonymous question submitters do not receive a persistent session cookie.
  • CSRF token — a security cookie used to protect form submissions from cross-site request forgery. This is a security requirement and contains no personal data.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics cookies.

7. Third-Party Services

This platform integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business API) — used to deliver questions to speakers. Privacy Policy
  • Render.com or Namecheap — web hosting provider where the application runs. Your data is stored on servers operated by our hosting provider.
  • Google Fonts & Bootstrap CDN — used for typography and UI styling. These services may log your IP address as part of standard CDN operation.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any third party for commercial purposes.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Know what information we hold — contact us and we will describe what is stored.
  • Delete a question you submitted — contact us with the approximate time and sermon, and we will remove it.
  • Object to processing — you may choose not to use this platform at any time.

Because submissions are anonymous, we cannot verify ownership of a specific question without additional context (e.g. exact text, time submitted, sermon). We will make reasonable efforts to honour deletion requests.

9. Children's Privacy

This service is open to all members of {{ CHURCH_NAME }}, including young people. We do not knowingly collect any additional personal data from children. Because no account or personal information is required to submit a question, the platform is safe for use by all ages. Parents or guardians with concerns may contact us using the details below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, please contact {{ CHURCH_NAME }} directly through your church's official communication channels or speak with a church administrator.

Questions About Your Privacy?
